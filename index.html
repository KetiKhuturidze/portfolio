  • Worked on monetization features (IAPs, rewarded ads) and progression.
  • Added meta systems such as customization, side loops and leaderboards.

  • Shot, staged, and produced ad creatives achieving $0.49 CPI, scaling UA profitably

UI FX System

Unity • C# • Reusable UI animation system

UI animation Sequencing Looping Appear / Disappear triggers DOTween-based
Watch demo video →

A modular UI effects system I built to add consistent motion across screens. It animates panels, images, and text using configurable effects that can run simultaneously or as sequences.

What it supports:

  • Spin, shake, move up/down, pop in/out, scale up/down, fade (images/text).
  • Multiple effects combined per element (simultaneous or chained).
  • Looping effects for attention/idle motion.
  • Trigger rules: play when a panel appears or right before it disappears.

Demo video shows both the result in UI and the configuration workflow.

Race Control XR

VR • Training Simulation • Motorsport

Marshal training Real-time incidents Flag procedures Track limits Immersive simulation Supervised training
Watch on Vimeo →

Race Control XR is a virtual reality training experience designed to teach and assess motorsport marshals through realistic race scenarios. Trainees operate from authentic marshal posts around a 3D racetrack, responding to incidents, flag procedures, and track-limit violations in real time.

Developed by an active racing marshal, the simulator recreates the pace and pressure of live race control—without the safety risks of trackside training. It provides a safe, repeatable, and immersive alternative for building crucial race management skills.

Features:

  • Real-time racing and simulated incidents
  • Accurate flag communication and incident-response training
  • Future: individual or group sessions with a Race Director role

Aquarium Shop

Mobile • Unity

Simulation Casual
Google Play →

Aquarium Shop was a bit different from our typical arcade/idle management titles. Beyond the core shop loop, the project required an optimized simulation layer that could support up to ~50 fish at runtime—each moving, reacting, and making simple decisions while remaining performant on mobile.

On this project, I:

  • Built and optimized a runtime fish simulation (up to ~50 active fish), including behaviors like avoiding the player during catch attempts.
  • Implemented AI customer behavior to simulate a real fish-shop flow: enter the shop, browse tanks, evaluate availability, choose a fish to buy, and wait for assistance when needed.
  • Developed supporting staff AI (cleaners and cashiers) to keep the shop loop readable and scalable.
  • Worked on player character animations and interactions such as swimming and jumping into the water.
